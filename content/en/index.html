Utility bills, calculated for you

You enter the meter readings — Platizhka computes the amount from your own tariffs, keeps the history of every month and reminds you when it is time to read the meters again. No internet, no account, no ads.

How tariffs are calculated →

Your tariffs, not someone else's

Consumption blocks, day/night zones, seasonal rates, fixed fees and combinations. Nothing is pre-filled from a national price list — you enter what is printed on your bill.

History that is never rewritten

Every saved month keeps its own calculation. Change a tariff later and past charges stay exactly as they were.

Several addresses

Flat, summer house, garage — each with its own services and tariffs.

Reminders

Once a month the app reminds you to take readings. You choose the time.

Works offline

There are no servers: all data stays on the device. Backups go to a file you keep yourself.

No subscriptions

Free, with no in-app purchases and no ad banners. There is nothing to switch off or cancel.

You can see where the money goes

The period report charts spending by month, with the average, the highest and lowest month and a year-over-year comparison. Not "what did I pay this time" but how it is changing.

Who it is for

For people who read their own meters and pay for electricity, water, gas and heating — in a flat, a house, a summer place or a rented home. If you still do the arithmetic in a notebook or a spreadsheet, this does the same thing without arithmetic mistakes and without losing last year's numbers.

The app ships no tariff rates, on purpose. Rates differ by region and supplier and they change. So you enter your own — the ones printed on your bill or in your contract. The app is responsible for the arithmetic, not for guessing your prices.

Making sense of tariffs

Usually the hard part is not the app but the billing itself: why consumption blocks do not work the way they look, and what a two-zone meter actually changes.
