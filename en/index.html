Utility bills, calculated for you

You enter the meter readings — Platizhka computes the amount from your own tariffs, keeps the history of every month and reminds you when it is time to read the meters again. No internet, no account, no ads.

How tariffs are calculated →

Any tariff

Single-rate, tiered, day/night zonal, multi-zone, seasonal and fixed fee — seven tariff types, combined ones included.

History that is never rewritten

Change a tariff or a price and past charges stay exactly as they were.

Any number of addresses

Your flat, your parents' house, a summer place, a garage — each with its own services and tariffs.

Reminders

At the start of each month the app reminds you to take the previous month's readings. You choose the time of day.

Works offline and anonymously

All data stays on the device. The backup is a file you keep yourself.

No subscriptions

Free, with no in-app purchases and no ad banners. There is nothing to switch off or cancel.

You can see where the money goes

The period report charts spending by month, with the average, the highest and lowest month and a year-over-year comparison. Not "what did I pay this time" but how it is changing.

Who it is for

For people who read their own meters and pay for electricity, water, gas and heating — in a flat, a house, a summer place or a rented home. If you still do the arithmetic in a notebook or a spreadsheet, this does the same thing without arithmetic mistakes and without losing last year's numbers.

You add the tariff rates yourself. Rates depend on your region and supplier, and they change over time. The app is responsible for the arithmetic, not for guessing your prices.

Making sense of tariffs

Usually the hard part is not the app but the billing itself. The blog explains how utility charges are calculated and what changes in tariffs and rules.
